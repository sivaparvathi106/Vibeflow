## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an immersive, high-fidelity dark-mode music player aesthetic. The visual narrative balances intense focus with vibrant energy, optimized for media consumption, deep listening, and exploration. 

- **Brand Personality:** Sleek, immersive, contemporary, and magnetic.
- **Target Audience:** Audiophiles, music creators, and everyday listeners seeking a polished, distraction-free environment.
- **Emotional Response:** Energy, focus, immersion, and effortless control.
- **Design Style:** Glassmorphism fused with modern dark-mode minimalism. Deep obsidian backgrounds contrast sharply with electric mint accents, while translucent frosted glass layers and subtle violet glows create a sense of depth and dimensionality.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for high-contrast dark-mode environments. The obsidian black foundation (`#09090B`) minimizes eye strain in low-light settings, allowing elevated charcoal surfaces (`#121215`) to establish a clear hierarchy. The electric mint accent (`#1ED760`) serves as the primary driver for interactive states, progress indicators, and active playback controls, while a subdued violet-indigo glow (`#6366F1`) adds atmospheric lighting to featured elements and background blooms. Text is uncompromisingly legible, utilizing pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for primary headings and muted zinc (`#A1A1AA`) for secondary metadata and inactive states.

## Typography

The typography system combines **Google Sans Code** for headlines to impart a modern, code-infused precision, with **Inter** for body and label text to maintain a neutral, highly legible, and utilitarian foundation. Letter spacing is slightly adjusted on larger headlines to enhance modern display aesthetics, while smaller labels feature increased tracking for superior legibility at micro scales. 

Headlines larger than 32px adapt gracefully on mobile viewports to prevent layout breaking, prioritizing scanning efficiency for track lists, artist profiles, and album headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a **12-column fluid grid** system optimized for desktop application views, collapsing seamlessly into single-column flows on mobile devices. Generous spacing rhythms allow content to breathe, utilizing 24px gutters and 32px outer canvas margins on desktop to prevent visual clutter.

- **Mobile (< 768px):** 16px outer margins, single-column stacked layout, collapsible navigation drawers.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 24px outer margins, 2-column split view for library browsing and main stage content.
